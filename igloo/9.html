Ireland 2036

The real island. Terrain and sea-floor depth from satellite survey data, satellite imagery draped over it, and every place — the Cliffs of Moher, the Liffey, Arklow Bank — at its true longitude and latitude. Underfoot, the same island as a lit relief map inside a ring that counts 2026 to 2036, and lines of light that run from the floor straight up the walls. The flight is slow and always forward. A wave of light leaves Dublin, and Ireland 2036 takes shape around you; in the last seconds the map settles onto the real island below.

Margin masks a projector that overshoots the floor. Keystone corrects an off-axis mount. O still rotates the world; the dial stays put. The light lines follow the room, so enter its real size on the start screen and they meet the walls exactly.
