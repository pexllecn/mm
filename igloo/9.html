Ireland 2036

The real island. Terrain and sea-floor depth from satellite survey data, satellite imagery draped over it, and every place — the Cliffs of Moher, the Liffey, Arklow Bank — at its true longitude and latitude. Underfoot, the same island as a lit relief map inside a ring that counts 2026 to 2036. At every chapter a circle of light spreads across the floor, squares itself to the room and climbs all four walls as one level line, with a shimmer of light in its wake. The flight is slow and always forward. At 1:16 the sweep for 2036 carries the new decade’s light up the walls, and in the last seconds the map settles onto the real island below.

Footage library

One clip per scene. Each clip wraps around all four walls as a seamless panorama and pools across the floor as a moving reflection. Clips save with the show, so the Igloo PC picks them up live. Scenes without a clip show the live 3D world.

Room controls

If Dublin looks sandy in the last act, lower Grade warmth and City light at night. Light lines sets the cove line, the year lines and the sweeps of light together. With the box above ticked (the default) the show steps the world down from 2048 to 1536 px per face if it cannot hold 60 fps.

Margin masks a projector that overshoots the floor. Keystone corrects an off-axis mount. O still rotates the world; the dial stays put. The light lines follow the room, so enter its real size on the start screen and they meet the walls exactly.

The pad moves through one chord per act. Chimes mark each caption, each year the dial reaches, and the map's heartbeat.
